Immortal Affinity

Otherworld Crystals earned through Battle of Asteria can be spent in the Otherworld Shop on gifts for your Immortals. Choose gifts carefully: the right match helps you make better use of your crystals.

Get More from Your Crystals

Matching an element-specific gift to the right Immortal is generally more efficient than using it on an unrelated element. Universal gifts offer flexibility and are worth considering for Immortals whose preferred gifts are harder to obtain, including Holy, Shadow and Chaos.

Don't assume that a more expensive gift is automatically better value. Compare the Affinity gained against its crystal cost, and check the in-game preview before confirming.

Daily Shop Limits

The Otherworld Shop screenshot shows these daily purchase limits: Spiritfont Nectar (1), Starweave Silk (3), Letter of Blessing (5), the 50-crystal gifts (3 each), and the 30-crystal gifts (5 each). The Basic Resources Optional Chest costs 20 crystals with a daily limit of 10, but it is not an Affinity gift.

Offers, prices and limits may change, so treat the shop itself as the final reference.
