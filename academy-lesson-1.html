Crimson Oak Academy · Lesson 1

The First Lesson

The easiest way to grow is to participate. Many of the resources, items and materials you need to strengthen your Immortals, marches and castle come from events where we work together as an alliance or server.

Malena's Ambition

Participate actively whenever the event is running. The rewards and event currency give you access to valuable items and materials that help strengthen your Immortals.

Contention of Relics

Taking part in CoR earns rewards while helping the alliance and server achieve their objectives. Even if you are not one of the strongest players, participating matters.

Frostborne

Frost is a team effort. Building, gathering, fighting, supporting and completing objectives all contribute to progress, and that progress brings rewards that help your account grow.

You don't need to be strong to participate. You participate to become strong.

Show up. Take part. Collect the rewards. Grow stronger. Then help the next player do the same.
