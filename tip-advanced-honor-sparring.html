Advanced Honor Sparring

Want to earn Honor with an alliance partner without damaging their castle's Prosperity? Try the Relocation Technique: defend a temporary relocation site instead of your main castle.

How the Relocation Technique Works

During a castle relocation, a temporary relocation site appears at the destination. You can garrison marches there, and your sparring partner can attack that site. In Crimson Oak testing, this allowed Honor farming without Prosperity damage to the castle.

This is a coordinated technique for consenting sparring partners, not an invitation to attack other players' relocation sites.

Set Up the Spar

  1. Coordinate with your partner. Agree who will defend, who will attack and when to stop.
  2. Adjust the defending marches. Remove dragons from marches you want to use as targets, such as March 3 and March 4. If necessary, review skills too, so your partner can win the fights.
  3. Start relocating your castle to a position on the opposite side of your sparring partner.
  4. Wait for the first transport cart to reach the destination and the temporary relocation site to appear.
  5. Garrison the temporary site with the marches prepared for sparring.
  6. Let your partner attack the relocation site and earn Honor. Review battle reports and adjust the defending marches if needed.

Make the Most of Honor

The attacking player can use the Rank Advancement talent in the War talent tree to increase Honor earned from winning attacks, where available and active. The defending player does not gain that attacking bonus simply by garrisoning the site.

Plan around both Bloody Rage and relocation cooldowns. Bloody Rage is commonly described as a 10-minute timer that can be refreshed by attacking. Relocation restrictions can vary with the action and game state, so check the timers shown in your own game rather than relying on a fixed number here.

What to Watch Out For

The aim is to avoid Prosperity damage to the main castle, not to make battles free of all costs. Troop losses, wounded troops, healing resources and other battle consequences still deserve attention. Check your reports and stop if the results are not what you expected.

New to sparring? Start with Academy Lesson 3 — Sparring before trying this more advanced method.

The Crimson Oak takeaway: Prepare weaker defending marches, garrison the temporary relocation site, and let your partner attack it for Honor. Use Rank Advancement on the attacking side, and watch your timers and battle reports.
