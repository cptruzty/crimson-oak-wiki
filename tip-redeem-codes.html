How to Redeem Gift Codes

Infinity Kingdom occasionally offers free rewards through gift codes. Redeeming them only takes a few taps once you know where to look.

Tap your avatar in the upper-left corner of the game screen to open your Lord Info.

Select Settings at the bottom of Lord Info, then choose Redeem.

Enter your gift code and tap Confirm. If the code is valid and eligible for your account, follow the game's instructions to claim your reward.
