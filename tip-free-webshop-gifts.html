Free Gifts from the Web Shop

Did you know that Infinity Kingdom's official GTarcade Web Shop offers free gifts? You don't need to buy anything. Sign in with the account linked to your game and claim the free packs.

3,000 Gems

Potentially free in 30 days from two daily packs, four weekly claims and one monthly claim. And that's before counting the other useful items!

Why AP and SP Matter

The daily gift also includes a Small AP Recovery and a Small SP Recovery. AP is used for many activities outside your city, while SP supports other game actions. Both are easy to run short of, especially when you're active. Over 30 days, claiming this pack every day gives you 30 Small AP Recovery items and 30 Small SP Recovery items, in addition to the Gems and other rewards.

How to Claim Your Gifts

  1. Open the official GTarcade Web Shop.
  2. Sign in with the account linked to your Infinity Kingdom character.
  3. Choose Infinity Kingdom and find the free offers under Points Pack.
  4. Select Redeem on each available free pack.
  5. The items are sent directly to your game account.

You don't need to purchase a paid pack to claim these free gifts.

The Crimson Oak Reminder

Two daily packs add up to 70 Gems per day. Over 30 days, that's 2,100 Gems. Add four weekly packs (400 Gems) and one monthly pack (500 Gems) for a total of 3,000 Gems. Don't leave those rewards unclaimed.
