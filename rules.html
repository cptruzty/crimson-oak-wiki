Rules & Consequences

The rules and agreements on Server 538 apply equally to everyone. Their purpose is to protect cooperation between alliances and resolve violations fairly and consistently.

First Offense

Warning

The player is informed of the violation and receives a warning.

Second Offense

Demotion & Restitution

The player is demoted and must return or compensate for any resources taken as a result of the violation.

Third Offense

Removal

The player is removed from the alliance.

The same process applies to everyone.

Rank, alliance or player status does not change the consequences. The purpose is consistency, fairness and respect for the agreements that keep Server 538 working together.
