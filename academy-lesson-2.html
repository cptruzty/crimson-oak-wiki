Parking 101

A good hive is more than a group of cities standing close together. It allows us to fit more players around an important location, move and reinforce efficiently, and organize ourselves so everyone knows where to go.

Why Parking Matters

Every castle takes up space. If we park randomly, small gaps quickly become unusable and the hive spreads farther than necessary. Good parking uses the available space efficiently and keeps the alliance together.

Parking Around a City

A normal city occupies a 2 × 2 block area, so parking is fairly simple. Castles can be arranged in a regular grid around the city with very little wasted space.

Parking Around a Relic

Relics are different. A relic occupies 2.5 × 2.5 blocks, which means the same grid no longer fits. To build a tight hive around a relic, the parking pattern gradually shifts and forms a spiral.
